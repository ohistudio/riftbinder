# Demo voiceover

About sixty seconds at a natural pace. Paced to the `deck-building` LEAF run:
menu, wall picks, the scan, Ask and Price, more picks, the deck sheet. Plain
text below is what to paste into ElevenLabs.

---

This is RiftBinder — a spatial deckbuilder for the Riftbound card game, built for Snap Specs.

Building a deck usually means a browser, a spreadsheet, and a box of cards on the desk beside them. RiftBinder makes this easier spatially by keeping your collection and your deck in the room with you, where the cards already are.

It knows your collection and lets you build decks from it. It even scans the card in front of you, adding it straight to your digital collection. You can ask questions about the card, and even get an estimate of its market value.

Every card lands on a sheet laid out like a real decklist: main deck, runes, sideboard. The rules check themselves as you go, and the deck tells you the moment it's legal.

And a friend in the same room can build it with you.

Every line of this was built with CLAD — the interface, the scanning, the assistant, and the simulated hand you've been watching, which is a LEAF scenario the agent wrote to test its own work.
